Privacy Policy — Kaufman Assistant Alerts

Effective date: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Glenn Kaufman ("I," "me," or "my") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with Kaufman Assistant Alerts, a personal text messaging (SMS/MMS) and voice program operated by Glenn Kaufman as an individual. The program lets my personal assistants send and receive notifications, reminders, and replies with me and with people I personally invite.

1. Information I collect

When you take part in Kaufman Assistant Alerts, I may collect:

  • Your mobile phone number.
  • Message content you send to or receive from the program's numbers (text messages, MMS attachments, and voicemails or call recordings, if any).
  • Message and call metadata, such as dates, times, delivery status, and opt-in or opt-out status.
  • Your name, if you give it to me.

I do not collect payment information through this program, and I do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

2. How I use your information

I use your information only to:

  • Send and receive the assistant messages and calls you have agreed to, such as notifications, reminders, and replies.
  • Keep a record of your consent (opt-in) and honor opt-out (STOP) requests.
  • Respond to HELP requests and other questions.
  • Keep the program secure and working properly.

I do not use your information for advertising, marketing, or selling anything.

3. Sharing of information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

I share information only with service providers needed to run the program, and only so they can deliver it. The main provider is Twilio Inc., which carries the texts and calls between the program's numbers and mobile carriers. Service providers are only allowed to use the information to provide their services to me.

I may also disclose information if the law requires it, for example to respond to a valid subpoena or court order, or to protect someone's safety.

I do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

4. Data retention

I keep phone numbers, consent records, and message history only as long as needed to run the program, keep a record of your consent or opt-out, and meet legal or carrier requirements. After you opt out, I keep a record of your number and opt-out so you are not messaged again. Message content is deleted when it is no longer needed.

5. Security

I use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, including access limited to me and my assistants, password-protected and two-factor-protected accounts, and encrypted connections provided by my service providers. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but I work to protect your information.

6. Your choices

  • Opt out: Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. You will get one final message confirming you are unsubscribed.
  • Help: Reply HELP for help.
  • Access or deletion: Email me at the address below to ask what information I have about you or to ask me to delete it.

7. Changes to this policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top will show when it was last changed.
